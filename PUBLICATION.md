# Publication policy

This public companion is a research record, not a product certification or claim of independent validation.

Before publishing a case or aggregate finding:

1. Freeze a versioned protocol before scored trials.
2. Verify permission to publish every source and response, and remove personal or confidential material.
3. Preserve run metadata including exact model ID, prompt, settings, date, evidence version, and response hash.
4. Keep answer keys out of model prompts and remove model identity from blinded evaluator review.
5. Record independent human review, unresolved disagreements, adjudication, and corrections.
6. Report denominators, missing responses, uncertainty, and known limitations.
7. Review proposed release against private prompts, scoring internals, credentials, and repository history.
8. Receive founder approval for the specific release.

Corrections must link to the prior record and explain the change. Never silently rewrite a reported result. Avoid ranking providers from small exploratory samples or describing Veritas as proven or production ready.
