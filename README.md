# Veritas — public research record

Veritas is an alpha-stage project exploring ways to audit claims in AI-generated responses against supplied evidence. We are developing a traceable workflow for recording claims, uncertainty, disagreements, human corrections, and known failures.

## Current status

Batch 1 is reported human-verified closed in the internal project records. That milestone concerns the integrity of the recording and correction workflow. Its underlying records are not published here, and it does not establish evaluator accuracy, calibration, commercial value, or superiority over other systems.

Batch 2 is being designed as a controlled cross-model study. Its protocol is a draft until a numbered version and commit are frozen before scored runs. There are no published Batch 2 results yet.

## What we plan to share

Versioned test protocols, original or properly licensed evidence packets, definitions and denominators for reported measures, selected response and audit records after review, human adjudications, correction notices, and limitations. Private evaluator prompts, proprietary implementation, credentials, personal data, and unreleased internal ledgers are outside this repository.

The published materials may allow others to inspect individual cases and judgments. They will not by themselves enable independent reproduction of a closed evaluator.

## Principle

Failure is useful only when it becomes traceable correction.

## Releases

No benchmark claim should be treated as verified until its protocol, data provenance, human adjudication, and limitations are published together. Follow the [publication policy](PUBLICATION.md) for release criteria.
